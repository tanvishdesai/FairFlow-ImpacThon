# Conference Shortlist for FairFlow

Date checked: 2026-04-28

## Best overall fit

### HITA2026

- Venue: Navi Mumbai, India
- Dates: 30-31 October 2026
- Submission deadline: 1 August 2026
- Mode: Hybrid
- Proceedings: Springer LNNS
- Indexing stated on site: Scopus indexed proceedings
- Why it fits:
  - India-based
  - hybrid participation
  - enough time to run experiments and write the paper
  - broad enough to accept a practical fairness-control paper

Official site: https://hita.stemrs.in/

## Stronger IEEE-flavored backup

### ICAIA 2026

- Venue: New Delhi, India
- Dates: 19-20 November 2026
- Full paper deadline: 31 May 2026
- Proceedings target: IEEE Xplore
- Site notes: IEEE conference ID shown, Scopus indexed claimed on conference page
- Why it fits:
  - India-based
  - stronger IEEE branding than HITA
  - realistic only if the writing and experiments move quickly in May and June

Official site: https://icaia-msit.in/

## Lower-travel IEEE backup with remote presentation option

### ICNGIS 2026

- Venue: Kottayam, Kerala, India
- Dates: 19-21 November 2026
- Draft paper deadline: 30 May 2026
- Proceedings: submitted to IEEE Xplore
- Presentation note on site: provision for online presentation for international and out-of-state participants
- Why it fits:
  - India-based
  - cheaper travel than overseas options
  - remote presentation support is explicitly mentioned

Official site: https://icngis2026.rit.ac.in/

## Near-India international option

### IAIT 2026

- Venue: Bangkok, Thailand
- Dates: 17-19 June 2026
- Hard submission deadline: 15 May 2026
- Mode stated on site: onsite
- Proceedings: ACM ICPS
- Indexing stated on site: Scopus, Web of Science, Ei Compendex
- Why it fits:
  - geographically closer than Europe or the US
  - good topic match for trustworthy AI
  - less ideal than the India options because it is onsite and the deadline is soon

Official site: https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/

## Recommendation

If the goal is to maximize realism against your timeline, budget, and travel preferences:

1. Target HITA2026 as the primary venue.
2. Keep ICAIA 2026 as the stronger-brand fallback if the experiments finish early enough.
3. Keep ICNGIS 2026 as the IEEE backup with lower travel burden.
